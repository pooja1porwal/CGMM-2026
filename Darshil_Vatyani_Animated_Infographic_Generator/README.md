# InfoMotion — Animated Infographic Studio (Light Mode)

> 🚀 **Live Demo**: [https://infomotion.vercel.app](https://infomotion.vercel.app)

A modern, high-aesthetic web application for converting statistics, structured data, and research into animated visual presentations. Designed from the ground up for a refined **Light Mode** aesthetic reminiscent of *The Financial Times*, *The New York Times*, *Stripe*, and high-end creative studios.

## ✨ Key Capabilities

### 1. 🎨 Refined Light Mode Themes
- **Editorial Gazette (News & Journalism)**: Warm eggshell paper tones (`#FAF7F0`), classical serif headers, thin ink borders, vermillion and deep navy accents.
- **Neo-Modern SaaS (Business & Tech)**: Crisp porcelain (`#F8FAFC`), modern geometric sans, vivid indigo & emerald accents, subtle frosted cards with elevation.
- **Vibrant Pop (Digital Marketing & Social)**: High-energy contrast, lemon yellow, coral pink, electric cyan, playful springs.
- **Nordic Minimal (Education & Science)**: Earthy stone, warm taupe, sage green, and charcoal with quiet, confident academic precision.
- **Swiss Bauhaus (Data Architecture)**: High-contrast black typography, geometric grid patterns, primary red/blue Bauhaus accents.

### 2. 📊 8 Animated Visualization Engines
- **Metric Big Numbers & Rolling KPI Counters**: Eased rolling counters with custom prefixes (`$`), suffixes (`M`, `%`, `x`), and positive/negative trend pills.
- **Staggered Animated Bar Charts**: Bar height emergence, hover glow, custom target lines, and value chips.
- **Radial Donut & Ring Charts**: SVG `stroke-dashoffset` circular sweep animations, centered highlight statistics, and interactive legend with percentages.
- **Bezier Trend Curve Charts**: Animated SVG curve draw-in, shimmering area gradients, and glowing data nodes.
- **Versus / Comparison Showdowns**: Head-to-head animated progress comparison bars with winner badges.
- **Chronological Milestone Roadmaps**: Connected step roadmaps with pulsing checkpoints, dates, and checkmarks.
- **Tapered Conversion Funnels**: Multi-tier conversion funnels with drop-off markers and conversion percentages.
- **Multi-Dimensional Metric Grids**: 4-card metric matrix with animated icons and deltas.

### 3. 🎬 Multi-Scene Storyboard & Motion Player
- **Interactive Scrubber Bar**: Scrub with millisecond timecode display.
- **Speed Controls**: `0.5x`, `1.0x`, `1.5x`, and `2.0x`.
- **Smooth Scene Transitions**: Morph, Push, Fade, Zoom, and Wipe.
- **Aspect Ratios**:
  - `16:9` Widescreen (Desktop & Presentation)
  - `9:16` Vertical (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)
  - `1:1` Square (LinkedIn & Carousel posts)
- **Zero-Dependency Web Audio Synthesizer**: Subtle tactile ticking as numbers roll, and soft whooshes on scene transitions.

### 4. 🤖 Data Authoring & Google Gemini AI Generator
- **Google Gemini API**: Native integration with Google's flagship models:
  - `gemini-3.6-flash` (Default Flagship)
  - `gemini-2.5-flash`
  - `gemini-2.0-flash`
  - `gemini-1.5-flash` / `gemini-1.5-pro`
- **Interactive Data Form**: Real-time reactive editing of values, labels, metrics, and categories.
- **Spreadsheet / CSV Editor**: Real-time spreadsheet editor with live CSV import and export.
- **4 Built-in Industry Decks**:
  1. *Business*: Q3 Enterprise SaaS Financial Diagnostics
  2. *Education*: The Human Brain vs Modern Supercomputing
  3. *News*: The Great Clean Power Shift (2015–2025)
  4. *Marketing*: Viral Omnichannel Growth & ROAS Report

### 5. 🚀 Export & Presenter Tools
- **Presenter Mode**: Full-screen presentation with keyboard shortcuts (`Spacebar`, `Arrows`, `Esc`) and speaker talking notes.
- **PNG Slide Snapshot**: 2x retina snapshot download.
- **WebM Video Recording**: Real-time canvas recording stream.
- **JSON Project Export/Import**: Save and share entire infographic projects.
